# Employee Training Management System

A full-stack web-based system developed during my Cooperative Training (COOP) at **Yanbu Municipality** to manage employee training course requests, review, approval, and nomination workflows.

## Project Overview

The system was developed to streamline the process of requesting and managing training courses for employees.

It supports a multi-level approval workflow where training requests are reviewed by the employee's direct manager, followed by the HR manager, and then proceed to the nomination stage for final submission.

The project idea was initially proposed by the organization, and I translated the requirements into a complete technical solution through system analysis, design, technology selection, development, testing, documentation, and deployment.

## System Workflow

The main workflow includes:

1. **Employee** submits a training course request.
2. **Direct Manager** reviews the request and approves or rejects it with a reason.
3. Approved requests are forwarded to the **HR Manager**.
4. The **HR Manager** reviews and processes the request.
5. Approved requests proceed to the **Nomination** stage.
6. The nomination is finalized for submission to the relevant training institution.
7. Employees can track the status and actions related to their requests.
